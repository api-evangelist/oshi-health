Fall is the time for cozy comfort foods. But seasonal treats can sometimes come with unwanted symptoms like bloating, diarrhea, cramping, and heartburn—especially if you live with a digestive condition like irritable bowel syndrome (IBS) or gastroesophageal reflux disease (GERD). You don’t have to choose between enjoying fall and taking care of your gut.
