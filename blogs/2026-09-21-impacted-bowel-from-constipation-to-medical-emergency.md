Constipation can feel like a nightmare while you wait for your gastrointestinal tract to pick up the slack and get things moving. But sometimes the issue goes deeper than just having a momentarily sluggish colon. Sometimes stool gets stuck in the rectum or colon, leading to an impacted bowel.
